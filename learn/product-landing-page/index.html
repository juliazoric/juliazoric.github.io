A heartbeat for the machine in the corner

Your home server is quiet until it isn't. Nervous watches it, notices when something stops answering, and tells you before you notice yourself.

What it does

Watches

Checks your services on a schedule you set. Not a ping — an actual request that proves the thing works.

Notices

Tells the difference between a slow answer and no answer. Retries before it decides something is broken.

Tells you

One message to the place you already read. No dashboards to remember, no app to install.

How it works

One small process, a handful of checks, and a queue on disk so a restart never loses an alert. It is meant to be boring: the interesting part of monitoring is the day it tells you something you did not know.
