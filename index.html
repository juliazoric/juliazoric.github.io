What I do

Most of my work is unglamorous and that is the point. A bot that answers in a group chat, a service that survives a restart at three in the morning, a page that loads on a bad connection. I care about the parts that fail quietly: the error nobody logs, the assumption nobody checked.

Services

Python daemons under systemd, with logging that tells the truth about what happened. No framework where the standard library will do — the whole of one of my bots runs on the standard library alone.

Bots

Telegram bots with real behaviour: memory, moderation, and a personality that holds. One of them lives in a group chat, answers only when called, and argues with advertising posted by other bots.

Web

Hand-written HTML and CSS. No framework for a page that does not need one. This page is the example: responsive, dark-mode aware, and readable on paper.

Now

Learning web development properly, from the foundations up — the slow way, with the certificate to match. Building things of my own and keeping notes on what breaks.

  • Technical Documentation Page — 22 passing
